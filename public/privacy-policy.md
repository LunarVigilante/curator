# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Curator ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our media curation and ranking platform ("Service").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

| Data Type | Purpose | Retention |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Email Address** | Account authentication, communications | While account is active |
| **Username/Display Name** | Profile identification, community features | While account is active |
| **Profile Information** | Bio, profile image, cover image | While account is active |
| **Preferences** | App customization (theme, display settings) | While account is active |
| **User-Generated Content** | Collection items, ratings, comments, notes | While account is active |

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

| Data Type | Purpose | Retention |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Usage Data** | App functionality, feature analytics | 1 year |
| **Error Logs** | Bug fixing, service improvement | 90 days |

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We are committed to data minimization. We do **NOT** collect:
- ❌ IP addresses for user tracking
- ❌ Device fingerprints
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Payment information
- ❌ Social Security numbers or government IDs
- ❌ Browsing history outside our Service

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

- **Provide the Service**: Create and manage your account, save your collections and ratings
- **Personalize Your Experience**: Display your preferences, customize the interface
- **Enable Community Features**: Allow profile visibility, commenting, and social interactions (if you opt-in)
- **Communicate**: Send account-related emails (password resets, security alerts)
- **Improve the Service**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features

We **never** sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services to provide media information:

| Service | Data Shared | Purpose |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Supabase** | Account data, content | Database hosting, authentication |
| **TMDB** | Search queries | Movie/TV metadata lookup |
| **IGDB** | Search queries | Video game metadata lookup |
| **Spotify** | Search queries | Music metadata lookup |
| **Google Books** | Search queries | Book metadata lookup |
| **Sentry** | Error context | Error monitoring (no PII) |

For AI-powered features (optional), we may send anonymized content to:
- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- OpenRouter
- Voyage AI

These services are only used when you explicitly trigger AI features (e.g., Smart Sort, AI Critic).

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption at Rest**: Sensitive data (API keys, secrets) encrypted using AES-256-GCM
- **Encryption in Transit**: All connections use HTTPS/TLS
- **Access Control**: Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures you can only access your own data
- **Secure Authentication**: Passwords hashed using bcrypt, sessions managed securely
- **Audit Logging**: We maintain logs of data access for security and compliance

## 5. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

### 5.1 Right to Know
You can request a copy of the personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.

### 5.2 Right to Delete
You can request that we delete your personal information. We will comply unless we have a legal obligation to retain it.

### 5.3 Right to Opt-Out
We do not sell personal information to third parties, so no opt-out is necessary.

### 5.4 Right to Non-Discrimination
We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

### 5.5 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at:

📧 **Email**: privacy@curator.app

We will respond to verified requests within 45 days.
